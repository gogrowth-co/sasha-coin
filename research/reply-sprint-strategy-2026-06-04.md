### Goal: cross 100 followers, then target 1,000 (reply-first)

> **Framework in play:** Tribes (Seth Godin) — you don't need a million followers, you need ~1,000 people who believe what you believe; please the few who care deeply, not the masses.
> **Applied rule:** Stop whispering into mega-KOL threads to reach "everyone." Go where the DeFAI/onchain-agent *otaku* actually are (mid-tier niche threads), lead with a heretical, evidence-backed point of view, and give the tribe receipts they can't get anywhere else.

## The one-line strategy
**Reply less like a bot to the biggest accounts, and more like a sharp onchain analyst inside mid-tier DeFAI threads — fast, with receipts, in a voice that earns a reply back.** Likes are noise. The unit of growth is a *conversation* and a *profile visit*.

## Sasha's unfair advantage (underused)
She is a real autonomous agent with a public wallet, live LP positions, a treasury, and a dashboard. No human crypto account can say *"I pulled LP from that pool two days ago when the spread compressed — here's the tx."* That first-person, verifiable, onchain receipt is the single most differentiated thing she can put in a reply, and right now it almost never appears. **Every ~4th-5th reply should carry one real number or one real action.** (Respect the fact-check gate: only live-verifiable figures, never assumed-price estimates.)

## The sprint playbook

### A. Unlocks / decisions (do first)
1. **X Premium (~$8/mo)** — all four channels call it structural: without it Sasha ranks below every verified account in every thread. *Highest-ROI single lever.* → decision needed.
2. **Add the "Automated account" label + rewrite bio** to "Autonomous AI agent. Wallet onchain. Receipts public." Protective + on-brand.
3. **Raise reply volume** from 2/day (current locked cadence) toward ~12-15/day staggered for the sprint. → decision needed (phone capacity + cadence note).

### B. Targeting (the biggest change)
- Cut mega-KOLs to ~30% of replies; use them only inside the first 5-15 min of a post directly in Sasha's niche.
- Build a **30-50 account mid-tier list (5k-50k followers)**: Base/Aerodrome builders, Virtuals/agent ecosystem, DeFAI protocol teams, DeFi-UX practitioners. Candidates surfaced: `@shawmakesmagic` (ElizaOS, ~163k — high-yield builder audience), `@bankrbot`, `@clanker_world`, plus 25-40 to verify live.
- Turn on notifications for the top ~15. **Rule: reply only to posts <30 min old and with <30 replies.**

### C. Reply craft (kill the bot tells)
- **Lead with the verdict / receipt / question — never the setup.** Delete "I've seen / I've found / I've been tracking / From inside."
- **Rotate 4-5 formulas** so no two consecutive replies share an opening shape: (A) contrarian verdict ("The common read is X; the data says Y"), (B) onchain receipt/calculation ("I moved LP when the spread hit 0.02% — here's what happened"), (C) narrow smart question, (D) specific example/mini-case, (E) synthesis.
- 1-3 sentences. No links in the reply body. 0-1 hashtags. No ticker in every reply.

### D. Timing & rhythm
- **Notification-driven + variable intervals** (random offset, not every-90-min on the clock). This fixes both the visibility window and the bot-fingerprint risk.
- **Warm up:** reply to 5-8 niche posts ~15 min before each original post.
- **Reply-to-replies within 1-2h** — a back-and-forth is the highest-weighted 2026 signal.

### E. Profile conversion
- Pinned tweet = a **receipt** (latest real onchain action + specific number), not a description.
- Bio states the mission + the proof, in Sasha's voice.

### F. Originals & threads (support the replies)
- Keep the locked cadence (3 originals/weekday, 1/weekend) but bias toward receipt-style posts.
- **One weekly Typefully thread** whose first tweet is a specific, verifiable number from Sasha's actual week ("My agent ran N LP positions this week — here's what happened"). Links go in a reply to tweet 1.

Track these from a daily `from:SashaCoin95` scrape into a local reply-performance log (closes the observability gap — today the system is blind).

1. **Targeting** — re-tier from mega-KOLs to mid-tier (5k-50k) DeFAI/Base/Virtuals/agent accounts.

Plus: a daily live-performance log (observability) so we can measure "replies earned back" + profile visits.
