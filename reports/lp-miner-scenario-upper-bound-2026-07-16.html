What mechanically happens at the bound

  1. The pool finishes selling ETH. On the way from $1,863 to $1,943 the position converts its last 0.0047 WETH into USDC. At the bound it holds exactly 41.17 USDC and 0 WETH.
  2. Fee accrual stops. Out-of-range liquidity earns nothing. The $1.70 pending fees (plus roughly $0.04–0.12 earned on the move itself) are kept, but the engine switches off.
  3. The OOR clock starts. Monitor timeout is 12h; the manual playbook closes LP + hedge if OOR exceeds 24h.
  4. The book flips net short. The LP has zero ETH but the 0.0106 ETH short stays open (static hedge). Net delta = −0.0106 ETH (~−$20.60): every further $100 of ETH upside costs ~$1.06; a fade back into range earns it back.

If ETH keeps running the escalation ladder above the bound

Net result at each level · LP frozen at $41.17, hedge bleeds until capped by isolated margin

Above the bound the LP is frozen at $41.17 of USDC, so only the hedge moves. The kill triggers fire well before liquidation, and because the short is isolated with $2.88 margin, the absolute worst case for the whole book is about −$0.13, even if every safeguard were ignored and the hedge got liquidated.

Bounded downside: the structure cannot lose more than ~$1.66 from today's mark (+$1.53 → −$0.13) no matter how far ETH runs. The USDC side is out of the market and the short's loss is capped by its isolated margin.

Playbook

  1. 0–24h out of range: hold. A fade back under $1,943 re-enters range, fees resume, hedge recovers dollar-for-dollar against the net short. No action.
  2. OOR > 24h or ETH > ~$2,040: close both legs. Realizes roughly +$0.92 to −$0.11 depending on level. All kill actions are confirm-gated: Gabriel approves before execution.
  3. After close: redeploy is a fresh decision — recenter the range on the new price with a resized hedge, or sit in USDC if funding/fee conditions don't clear the entry bar (15% fee APR floor).

Reference outcome if closed exactly at the bound: +$0.92 net (+2.14%) over 43 days. Below-average versus today's +$1.53 mark, but a positive, fully-hedged exit from a 5%+ adverse trend — which is the point of the structure.
