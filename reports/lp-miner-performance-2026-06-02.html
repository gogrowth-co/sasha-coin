04The yield is AERO emissions, not swap fees

The position is staked in the Aerodrome gauge. Verified in lp-harvest.js: yield is claimed via gauge.getReward() and valued via gauge.earned() in AERO. The field labeled pendingFeesUsd is a misnomer.

  • Pending reward: $1.485 (≈ 3.93 AERO @ $0.378).
  • While staked, the LP earns AERO emissions and forfeits organic swap fees (they go to voters).

⚠ Emission-dependent

This is emission yield, not organic fee yield — the same distinction the Mantle signal fix was built around. Emission APR decays and AERO carries sell pressure. Strip it out and this position earns nothing organic while staked.

06What this actually says

  1. The dashboard overstates the result by ~4x. It reports +$2.05 by counting the hedge's +$2.006 mark-to-market gain as profit while carrying the LP at $45 flat. Marked to market, the real result is +$0.45.
  2. The hedge worked but under-covered. LP lost $3.05, the short recovered $2.006. The ~$1.04 gap is the LP's structural IL that a static, non-rebalanced hedge cannot recover.
  3. Profit is 100% emission-dependent. Strip the AERO and the unit is net negative (≈ −$1.02). The only reason it is green is emissions.
  4. Currently well delta-matched. LP holds 0.000437 cbBTC; hedge shorts 0.00043 BTC. Net delta near zero right now.
  5. Capital efficiency is poor. ~$13 of the footprint (idle hedge buffer + idle LP wallet) earns nothing.

07Verdict

★ The call

Mechanically sound, currently neutral, profitable only on emissions. Over 7 days the AERO ($1.485) barely outran the divergence loss after the hedge (−$1.04), netting about +$0.45. At $45 deployed, the absolute dollars are trivial and the idle-capital drag is large relative to the position.

The real decision is not "is it working" (it is, narrowly). It is: does AERO emission APR justify the IL plus the ~$13 of idle capital this unit ties up? Should the hedge rebalance dynamically (it under-covered on the way down)? And is this a demo or a position you intend to scale — because at this footprint, that answer decides everything else.
