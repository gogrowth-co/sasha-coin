The decision

Enter the Casper Agentic Buildathon, build to win. Foundation-grade and full chain-agnostic (Gabriel's call) — a settlement-adapter core with Casper as the flagship adapter that ships first, plus an EVM/Base proof adapter so multi-chain is real in code, not just claimed. Run through a council (5 advisors) and a Gary-Klein pre-mortem before any build.
Why now

Field is thin (6 BUIDLs, 1 formal team) and flat for days. 22-day runway. Casper is actively paying — $100K of the prize is sponsored x402 credits — to manufacture exactly this kind of agent.

Why us

Sasha is the only AI-agent persona entered and the only entrant who runs real DeFi. The "real project with socials + launch plans" criterion that others fake, she already is.

The hard truth

Casper is non-EVM WASM/Rust/Odra. Sasha's stack is 100% Solidity/JS — this is net-new Rust. Gated by a Day-0 spike on the hardest leg + Day-7/14 go/no-go.

The product — Sasha's x402 Agent Commerce Kit

Persona-facing: Sasha's Alpha Desk. Not "an agent that resells data over x402" (the field has 5+ of those). The agent's real DeFi book, made verifiable and payable. Four verbs:

PAY

Buys its own signals over x402

HTTP 402 → sign → settle. "I pay for the data I act on." Ref: make-software/casper-x402 client.

ACT

Runs a real testnet position

Manages yield/LP via the hosted cspr-trade-mcp (24 tools). The moat: real DeFi nobody else in the field is doing. [stretch]

ATTEST

Writes every decision on-chain

A Casper agent-identity + attestation contract (ERC-8004 pattern → Odra, rewritten fresh). Starting point: odradev/casper-x402-poc.

EXPOSE

Sells its verified yield over x402

RWA referent = a real position, not a synthetic label. A genuine external agent pays this endpoint — never a self-call.
