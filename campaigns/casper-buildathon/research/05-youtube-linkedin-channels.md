## Executive Summary

- Casper's largest owned community channels are Telegram (42,378 members main channel + 40,099 announcements channel) and Discord (est. 33,990 members), not YouTube or LinkedIn.
- YouTube is low-volume and low-growth: 28,500 subscribers, 113 videos total, zero new videos published May-June 2026. The channel shed 100 subscribers in the last 36 days.
- LinkedIn has only 3,702 followers — a small but professionally positioned audience. Content is governance/RWA/dev-tooling focused, with the 2026 buildathon actively featured.
- The blog on casper.network/news is the most active owned editorial channel, publishing multiple times per week in May-June 2026, with strong AI + RWA positioning. This is where narrative is being set.
- The Casper Manifest is a web page (not PDF) at casper.network/news + a recap of the May 20 X Space. It defines 9 technical initiatives for 4 audience types: developers, users, institutions, and machines. The machines track — powered by X402 micropayments — is the direct entry point for Sasha Coin's buildathon narrative.
- The Casper AI Toolkit (announced June 4, 2026) is the newest product signal and the most relevant to Sasha's agent-agent transaction positioning.
- No Substack or Beehiiv newsletter found. The newsletter is a hosted form on casper.network/newsletter with no subscriber count disclosed and no named platform.

### Casper Manifest

- **Format:** Not a PDF. The Manifest content was presented via a web-based X Space recap article at casper.network/news (the direct /casper-manifest URL returns 404, and the dedicated news article URL pattern also 404s — the content lives in the May 21, 2026 X Space recap article).
- **What it says:** A strategic roadmap organized around 9 protocol initiatives for 4 audience types:

  **For Developers:** EVM compatibility (end 2026), multi-VM architecture (Solidity + WebAssembly on same chain), native token registry.

  **For Users:** Gasless transactions, batch transactions (atomic multi-step), smart accounts with biometric auth and spending controls.

  **For Institutions:** Confidential transactions, ERC-3643 support for regulated tokenized assets, quantum-resistant keys (2027).

  **For Machines:** X402 payment infrastructure — AI agents pay for services and API calls via HTTP 402 payment requests without traditional billing cycles. This is the machine-to-machine economy track.

- **Buildathon connection (from Manifest):** Casper commits to sponsor up to $100,000 in X402 transactions during the buildathon. The emphasis is on agents that "transact" — not simple chatbots — operating within defined spending limits.

### Casper AI Toolkit (June 4, 2026)

The most relevant product for Sasha Coin's buildathon entry. Five components:

1. **x402 Facilitator** — machine-to-machine micropayment layer. Agents pay for services/API calls directly.
2. **MCP Integration** — grants agents direct access to ledger monitoring, state inspection, and blockchain operations.
3. **CSPR.trade** — DeFi MCP for optimal swap routes, liquidity pool comparison, token swaps, portfolio tracking.
4. **Odra Framework** — predictable sandbox for AI agents to generate and test smart contracts with reduced human intervention.
5. **CSPR.build Agent Skills** — middleware for wallet handshakes, transaction signing, and event routing at scale.

## 5. Strategic Implications for Sasha Coin Buildathon

**Where buildathon content gets amplified:**

1. **Telegram (main channel, 42,378 members)** — the largest active community. Any official buildathon content will be broadcast here. Sasha should monitor this channel for buildathon announcements.
2. **Telegram announcements (40,099)** — read-only broadcast. Official milestone announcements land here.
3. **Discord (~33,990 members)** — developer support hub. Builder-to-builder conversation happens here. Joining and being active in the Discord during the buildathon period is the highest-leverage community action.
4. **casper.network/news** — where new projects get featured in recaps. A strong submission gets editorial coverage.
5. **LinkedIn (3,702 followers)** — small but professionally positioned. A press mention here reaches enterprise validators and institutional contacts, not retail.
6. **YouTube (28,500 subscribers, zero 2026 uploads)** — a content vacuum. If Casper starts publishing buildathon demo videos or AI agent content, this channel will activate. There is no competition from Casper for AI agent YouTube content — this is open space.

**Content gap identified:** Casper has no YouTube content for AI agents, the AI Toolkit, or the Agentic Buildathon as of June 6, 2026. Zero videos published in 2026. The AI Toolkit launched June 4. This is a 48-hour window where Sasha Coin could be the first video content explaining or demoing the Casper AI Toolkit.

**Manifest alignment:** The X402 payment infrastructure (machines paying machines) is the exact narrative angle Sasha Coin's buildathon entry should anchor to. The Manifest explicitly positions this as Casper's differentiated layer for autonomous agent economies. Sasha as an autonomous agent that transacts on Casper directly embodies this vision.
