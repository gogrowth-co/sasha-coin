# Social Intelligence: Casper Network X/Twitter Footprint — 2026-06-06

## Executive Summary

- @Casper_Network (215,522 followers, verified organization) is running a high-frequency AI/agent campaign. Virtually every post in May-June 2026 ties to the Casper Agentic Buildathon, x402 payment protocol, AI toolkit launch, or the machine economy narrative.
- The buildathon buzz on X is real but thin. Only 8-12 unique accounts are posting about it outside Casper's official channels. Official posts get 88-121 likes; non-official amplifiers average 3-35 likes.
- The IBW x Casper relationship is a side-event activation, not a deep partnership. Casper drove all joint mentions. @IstanbulBlockWk (12,087 followers) posted zero dedicated Casper content.

- The x402 protocol is the narrative anchor. Phoenix Zero, the first submitted BUIDL, is explicitly x402-powered. This is Casper's clearest differentiation claim.
- A vote campaign will face a low-baseline audience. Casper's own posts max out at ~5.6K views. A Sasha entry would need to plug into the existing cspr.fans voting mechanism and get @Casper_Network amplification to matter.

## Strategic Implications

**For content strategy:**

- The x402 + AI agent narrative is the right hook. Every high-engagement Casper post uses these terms. Sasha should lean into "AI agent making autonomous x402 payments on Casper" as the positioning.
- Casper is explicitly positioning the chain as "the most comprehensive AI offering of any L1." Sasha entering the buildathon reinforces this claim and gives Casper an incentive to amplify.
- The @kenn_ronin tier list signal is important: hackathon-focused builder influencers are already aware of this event. Sasha's campaign should target that audience.

**For the vote campaign:**

- The cspr.fans voting mechanism requires direct engagement with the cspr.fans platform (DoraHacks-linked), not the @CSPRfans X account.
- The primary amplification path for votes is getting @Casper_Network to mention Sasha's entry. Their posts drive 2K-6K views. A single quote from them could double the visible vote count.
- @TimeIsMoneyHQ is the most active aligned amplifier (35 likes, 2.7K views on an IBW post). Engaging or tagging this account in buildathon content is a low-cost amplification lever.
- @kenn_ronin (15.5K views on tier list) is the highest-value cold target. Getting the Sasha entry on his radar before the deadline could drive meaningful developer awareness.
- Organic community buzz is essentially zero outside official channels. The vote campaign needs either @Casper_Network amplification or a targeted effort at 2-3 high-engagement accounts (@kenn_ronin, @TimeIsMoneyHQ, @mssteuer).

**For positioning:**

- There is no other AI agent persona entered in the buildathon based on current X data. Sasha as an autonomous AI agent entering a buildathon for AI agents is a self-referential concept that maps directly onto Casper's narrative. This is a strong angle.
- The "AI agent voting for itself" community story writes itself and gives cspr.fans something worth amplifying.
