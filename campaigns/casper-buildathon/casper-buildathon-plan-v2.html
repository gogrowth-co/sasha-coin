02

The product loop.

Sasha's Alpha Desk. Not "an agent that resells data over x402" (the field has 5+ of those). The agent's real DeFi book, made verifiable and payable. Four verbs in a continuous cycle.

PAY

Buys its own signals over x402

HTTP 402 → sign → settle. "I pay for the data I act on." Ref: make-software/casper-x402 client.

ATTEST

Writes every decision on-chain

Casper agent-identity + attestation contract (ERC-8004 pattern → Odra, rewritten fresh). Starting point: odradev/casper-x402-poc.

STRETCH
ACT

Runs a real testnet position

Manages yield/LP via hosted cspr-trade-mcp (24 tools). The moat: real DeFi nobody else in the field is doing.

EXPOSE

Sells its verified yield over x402

RWA referent = a real position, not a synthetic label. A genuine external agent pays this endpoint. Never a self-call.
