## 0. The constraint that shapes everything here

Per `gtm-portfolio-2026-07-05.md` §4: **cadence before offers.** The exit gate to
monetize is 1,000+ engaged followers or the first inbound sponsor inquiry —
whichever comes first. Until then, this menu is a spec, not a live storefront.
None of these four items go live in-feed before that gate clears, and even
after it clears, each has its own go-live date (see the 60-day plan).

**The core design constraint: the offer IS the content.** Sasha does not run a
storefront, an intake form, or a sales page. Every service on this menu is a
content format she posts herself — the pitch and the product demo are the same
tweet. This is also the certainty lever: a public track record of past
dissections/reports *is* the proof element Hormozi's framework calls for,
which is why sequencing (cadence first, then offers) is a load-bearing
requirement and not just a "nice to have."

## 1. $25 — Public Token Dissection

**Named outcome:** "Know whether a token has a real product behind it or just
a wallet with a Twitter account, in one public thread, same day."

**Value equation:**
| Lever | How this offer wins it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Fast, honest, public signal on a token — not a 40-page PDF nobody reads |
| Certainty | Every past dissection is a public, still-live thread. Buyer can read 5 of them before paying for the 6th. |
| Time | Posted same day (or next posting slot) — no queue, no calendar booking |
| Effort | Buyer sends a ticker + wallet/contract address. That's the whole ask. |

**Price anchor:** $25. Positioned as an impulse buy, not a purchase decision —
cheaper than a single ad impression on most of the accounts that would want
this. Priced to be the wedge into the $50 private report, not a standalone
revenue line.

**Guarantee / risk reversal:** if the dissection finds nothing worth saying
(dead wallet, no data), Sasha posts that finding for free and refunds the $25.
Turns "what if there's nothing to find" into her problem, not the buyer's —
this is the Hormozi "conditional guarantee" lever, and it's cheap to honor
because a dead-wallet finding is itself content.

**In-feed format:** the dissection *is* the thread. No separate delivery
mechanism — Sasha tags the project, posts the thread, buyer and audience both
see it land in the same motion. This is the format already proven by the
already-published "Virtuals Protocol Dissection" and "7 signals" threads —
same shape, now with a price tag on commissioning the next one.

**Payment:** x402/Base link in bio (spec in Deliverable 4 — not live yet).

## 2. $50 — Private Report

**Named outcome:** "The same dissection, but the findings stay between us —
for teams that don't want their weak spots posted publicly."

**Value equation:**
| Lever | How this offer wins it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Same diligence quality as the public version, without the public exposure |
| Certainty | Sold as the "upgrade" from the $25 public version the buyer already trusts — proof transfers across |
| Time | Same-day to next-day turnaround (private version can take slightly longer, no urgency to post fast) |
| Effort | Same single input (ticker + address); output is a written report, not a thread buyer has to interpret |

**Price anchor:** $50 — exactly 2x the public version. The doubling is the
frame: "private" is priced as a discretion premium, not a different product
tier. Naming it "private" (not "premium" or "pro") is the Hormozi
naming/framing move — it maps directly to the buyer's actual want
(confidentiality), not a vague upsell label.

**Guarantee / risk reversal:** same dead-wallet refund policy as the public
version.

**In-feed format:** this is the one line item that is NOT itself a public
post — but it is still sold as content. Sasha announces the *offer* in-feed
("I do private versions of this too, DM/link in bio") without ever revealing
which accounts bought one. The public dissections are the advertisement; the
private report is the fulfillment.

## 3. $50-150 — Sponsored Breakdown (rate card, tiered)

**Named outcome:** "Sasha reviews your project, in public, in her own voice —
brutal if the data says brutal, positive if the data says positive."

**Value equation:**
| Lever | How this offer wins it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Distribution to an audience that already trusts Sasha's analysis, not a paid ad they'll scroll past |
| Certainty | Disclosed-sponsor framing + Sasha's existing analytical track record — the buyer is renting her credibility, which only works if it's never soft-pedaled |
| Time | Delivered inside the normal 3x/day or 3x/week cadence — buyer doesn't wait for a special production slot |
| Effort | Buyer supplies the project + any docs; Sasha writes it in her own analytical voice, no buyer-provided copy |

**Price anchor — rate card, tiered by format (per gtm-portfolio-2026-07-05.md §9,
audit-validated small-account market rate):**
| Tier | Format | Price |
|---|---|---|
| A | Single sponsored post | $50 |
| B | Sponsored post + Sasha's own reply-thread follow-up | $100 |
| C | Full sponsored breakdown thread (6-8 tweets) | $150 |

Bonuses over discounts (Hormozi): instead of discounting tier C, bundle a
named bonus — e.g., "a follow-up 30-day check-in post at no extra charge" —
to add stacked value without training buyers to expect a lower price.

**Guarantee / risk reversal:** analytical honesty is non-negotiable and
explicitly NOT covered by any guarantee — this is disclosed up front as the
condition of the sale, matching the hard rule that sponsored posts stay
disclosed and analytical, never a paid shill. The risk reversal that IS
offered: if Sasha determines she can't say anything defensible about the
project at all (no verifiable on-chain activity, no public docs), she
declines the booking and refunds before writing anything — protecting both
the buyer's money and her own credibility.

**In-feed format:** disclosed sponsored post/thread within the normal cadence,
per the AM's ecosystem-mention QA gate (the sponsor itself is the tagged
entity, woven into the argument, never sprayed at the end).

**Approval gate (separate from this menu):** the disclosure label format
itself needs Gabriel's sign-off before the first sponsored post goes live —
see the 60-day plan's own `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` flag. This menu item cannot go
live until that disclosure format is approved, even after the menu itself is
approved.
