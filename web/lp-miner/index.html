How to read this dashboard
Dashboard guide
  1. Net result (hero):the marked-to-market net result on working capital. This is LP MTM change + hedge unrealized PnL + swap fees (uncollected, read on-chain) + AERO emissions + funding income minus gas. NOT the blended NAV. Working capital is LP basis deployed plus hedge margin.
  2. P&L decomposition:every component that adds or subtracts from the net result, labeled individually. LP MTM tells you how the pool rebalanced. Hedge PnL tells you how the short offset it. The yield line is AERO gauge rewards when the position is staked (organic swap fees then go to voters), or the swap fees the position collects directly when it is unstaked.
  3. Range bar (per position):the centerpiece. Shows the LP's configured price band with the current price of the pool's volatile asset as a marker. The purple band is the in-range zone. The marker turns red if price exits the range. Distance-to-floor is the most actionable risk signal right now.
  4. Setup column (per card):pair, chain, venue, fee tier, staked status, delta-neutral status, composition (the token split shows how much the pool has rebalanced).
  5. Performance column (per card):LP value vs. basis, divergence after hedge (the net IL), fees or emissions, hedge PnL, funding, liquidation distance, net result on working capital.
  6. Hedge section:the Hyperliquid perpetual short that offsets the LP's volatile-asset exposure. Entry, mark, unrealized PnL, liquidation price, margin, and funding rate. Positive funding = longs pay us.
  7. Capital book:where every dollar sits. Working capital (in the strategy) vs idle (quarantined, never part of the return calculation).
  8. Kill-switches:automated risk guardrails. Thresholds and armed status. Green means the miner is running normally.
FAQ
What is a delta-neutral LP?
The LP earns trading fees (or AERO emissions, when staked) on a pool that holds a volatile asset. But holding that asset inside an LP means the portfolio moves with its price. To cancel that, the strategy shorts the exact amount of the volatile asset on Hyperliquid. The LP earns yield; the short earns funding. Together the price exposure nets to zero.
Why does the hero show a loss? Is that bad?
The hero is marked-to-market: LP MTM change + hedge PnL + fees/emissions + funding. In a down-move the LP drifts toward the volatile asset and its value falls, while the short gains roughly the same amount, so the net IL after hedge is small. This is what a delta-neutral position looks like in a drawdown: the hedge absorbs most of the move, and fees plus funding decide whether the net is positive.
What is carry and where does yield come from?
Carry = trading fees and/or AERO emissions + funding received minus gas minus IL from range rebalancing. The strategy is positive-carry when price stays inside the range. If price moves a lot, IL/LVR grows and carry turns negative. That is what the kill-switches protect against.
Staked or unstaked: fees or AERO emissions?
It depends on where the LP NFT sits. Staked in Aerodrome's gauge, the position earns AERO emissions and forfeits organic swap fees to veAERO voters. Unstaked, the NFT sits in the agent's own wallet and collects swap fees directly, with no emissions. The dashboard labels whichever mode the live position is in, driven off the on-chain NFT owner.
What is "idle capital" and why is it separated?
Idle capital is cash sitting in the LP wallet or the Hyperliquid buffer that is not part of the active strategy. It earns nothing and is not included in the return denominator (working capital). Mixing it in would flatter the return percentage.
What does "funded" vs "unfunded" mean?
"Funded" means the reconciler queried the Aerodrome NFT contract and confirmed real on-chain liquidity. "Unfunded" means the state file says the position is open, but the chain disagrees, a mismatch that requires investigation. "Unverified" means the RPC call failed and the check is pending.
What happens if price goes out of range?
The LP earns zero fees while out of range. The monitor does not auto-recenter. It waits for mean-reversion: after 12 hours continuously out of range it sends an informational alert (hold and evaluate, no close). It only flags a kill on a real trend, when price moves 5% or more beyond the band, or when the hedge mark comes within 3% of its liquidation price. A kill is a flagged recommendation, never an automatic close. Any on-chain transaction needs Gabriel's confirmation.
What triggers the hedge drift adjustment?
As price moves within the LP range, the amount of the volatile asset in the pool changes, so the hedge can drift from the LP's exact exposure. An auto-rehedged position re-syncs when drift exceeds its threshold; this position runs a static hedge (set once at open), so drift is reviewed and corrected manually instead.
What is the funding kill switch?
If perpetual funding drops below -54.75% annualized for 3 consecutive 8-hour periods, holding the short costs more than the LP earns. The executor closes the hedge to stop the bleed. The LP continues running, but reverts to directional until funding normalizes.
Who controls the money?
Sasha is an autonomous AI agent. The kill-switches fire automatically. Capital movements above a minimum size require explicit confirmation from Gabriel before any on-chain transaction executes. This dashboard is read-only. No funds are held here.
Glossary
NAVNet Asset Value. Total capital footprint: LP deployed + idle wallet + Hyperliquid hedge margin. Secondary metric, never the hero.
Working capitalLP basis deployed + hedge margin used. The denominator for return %. Idle cash is excluded.
LP / CLMMLiquidity Position in a Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker. Capital deployed inside a specific price range. Earns nothing while out of range.
Delta-neutralThe volatile asset's price moves do not change total portfolio value. The LP's exposure to that asset is exactly offset by a Hyperliquid short of the same size.
AERO emissionsGauge rewards paid in AERO tokens for staking the LP NFT in Aerodrome's incentive contract. Not the same as swap fees.
Swap feesOrganic trading fees on the pool. Staked positions forfeit these to veAERO voters; an unstaked position collects them directly. The dashboard shows whichever applies to the live position.
Funding ratePeriodic payment between longs and shorts on a perpetual exchange. Positive rate = longs pay shorts (we receive). Negative rate = shorts pay longs (we pay).
Divergence after hedgeNet IL: LP MTM change + hedge uPnL. What remains after the hedge offset. The true impermanent loss cost.
CarryNet income: trading fees and/or AERO emissions + funding received minus gas minus IL from rebalancing. Strategy is +EV when carry is positive.
OOR (Out of Range)Current market price is outside the LP's configured band. No fees accrue while OOR. Sustained OOR (12h+) raises an informational alert; the monitor does not auto-recenter. A close is flagged only on a 5%+ distance move or a hedge-liquidation threat, and always needs Gabriel's confirmation.
Hedge driftGap between the LP's current exposure to the volatile asset and the Hyperliquid short size. Builds as price moves within range. An auto-rehedged position rebalances past its drift threshold; a static hedge is corrected manually.
Kill switchA risk rule that flags a position close or hedge removal when a threshold is breached: price 5%+ beyond the band (trend), hedge mark within 3% of liquidation, or extreme negative funding. The monitor only writes the signal and alerts. Execution requires Gabriel's explicit confirmation.
Gauge / stakingAerodrome Slipstream gauge contract that custodies the LP NFT and distributes AERO rewards. The NFT shows 0 in your wallet while staked.
Live-vs-state reconciledCross-checks every position against the actual blockchain every 5 min. No position is shown as funded unless the chain confirms it.
